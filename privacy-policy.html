AstroCaptain Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

AstroCaptain ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by Afterday. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using AstroCaptain you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information you provide

To give you astrology readings, the app may ask for details such as your name, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth. You choose what to enter. This information is used only to prepare the content you request inside the app.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the app, certain technical information may be collected by the app or by the services it relies on:

  • Device type, operating system version and app version
  • Crash reports and basic usage diagnostics
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Advertising ID, for showing and measuring ads

3. How we use information

  • To generate and display the readings and charts you ask for
  • To keep the app working, fix errors and improve it
  • To show advertisements that support the app
  • To respond when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising and third-party services

AstroCaptain displays ads through third-party providers, including Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. These providers may collect and use your advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, and may show personalised ads depending on your device settings. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

You can limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

5. Internet use

The app needs an internet connection to work. Requests made by the app travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS) wherever possible.

6. Data storage and retention

Details you enter are kept only as long as needed to provide the app's features. You can delete your saved profiles inside the app, or by uninstalling it, which removes locally stored data from your device. If you would like any data held on our side removed, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

AstroCaptain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

You may access, correct or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to its use. Where applicable, this includes your rights under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
